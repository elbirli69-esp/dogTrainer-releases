🐕 Dogaze — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Information We Collect

Dogaze collects the following information to provide our dog walking and social networking services:

  • Account information: Email address, display name, and profile photo when you sign in with Google or create an account.
  • Dog profiles: Dog name, breed, weight, age, and photos you choose to share.
  • Walk data: GPS location during walks (routes, distance, duration), accelerometer data for pull detection, and microphone data for bark/sound classification.
  • Social data: Posts, likes, comments, follows, and event participation.
  • Device data: Device model, OS version, and app version for crash reporting and analytics.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide real-time coaching during dog walks
  • Track walking history and progress over time
  • Enable social features (sharing walks, following users, community feed)
  • Report and display safety incidents on the community map
  • Improve the app through anonymized analytics
  • Send notifications about social activity and community events

3. Data Storage & Security

Walk data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted database. Social data is stored on Google Firebase (Firestore) with industry-standard security. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Location Data

GPS location is collected only during active walks and is used to map your route, detect nearby safety incidents, and match you with nearby dog walkers. You can choose whether to share walk routes publicly.

5. Microphone & Sensors

The microphone is used only during walks for on-device sound classification (barking, whining). Audio is processed locally using machine learning and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. Accelerometer data is used for pull/tension detection during walks.

6. Your Rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings
  • You can toggle profile and walk visibility (public/private)
  • You can use the app in offline mode without creating an account
  • You can export your walk history data

7. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, analytics, crash reporting)
  • OpenStreetMap (map tiles, no user data shared)
  • ONNX Runtime (on-device ML inference, no data leaves your device)

8. Children's Privacy

Dogaze is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes through the app.
